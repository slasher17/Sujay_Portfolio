01 — Profile

About me

I work where email security, detection engineering and automation meet — turning live spam and phishing campaigns into durable detections that scale.

Detection engineering

Author anti-spam filters and aggressive heuristic/REGEX rulesets on email headers and bodies, block malicious URLs and domains, and feed new techniques into Machine Learning, impersonation and spoof detection models.

Threat analysis

Analyse email headers and attachments end to end — dynamic detonation in the any.run sandbox plus static and dynamic file analysis with PE Studio, CFF Explorer and Wireshark.

Automation & quality

Python-driven automation of false-positive reproduction, scan requests and chronic-ticket creation — cutting manual effort and shortening turnaround time on customer escalations.
