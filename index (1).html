01 — Profile

About me

I work where email security, detection engineering and automation meet — turning live spam, phishing and malware campaigns into durable, low-false-positive detections that scale to hyperscale mail flow.

🛡

Signature & detection engineering

Author YARA, YACA and Snort signatures against malicious attachments, payloads and network patterns, alongside REGEX and heuristic anti-spam filters on email headers and bodies. Every rule is tuned for coverage without collateral false positives.

🎯

False-negative hunting

Run proactive FN hunting campaigns across mail-flow telemetry — surfacing threats that slipped past existing filters, clustering them into campaigns, and closing the gap with new detections and blocks.

🧬

Hash triage & blocking

Analyse VirusAnalysis (VA) hashes, verify verdicts through static and dynamic examination, and push confirmed malicious hashes to block — with clean reversal paths when a verdict is contested.

🔬

Malware analysis

Detonate attachments in the any.run sandbox and perform static and dynamic file analysis with PE Studio, CFF Explorer, Regshot and Wireshark to extract durable indicators.

⚙️

Automation

Python 3 and API-driven automation of false-positive reproduction, scan requests and chronic-ticket creation — cutting manual effort and shortening escalation turnaround.

🤝

Escalation ownership

Resolve customer escalations inside SLA, collaborate with efficacy and technology teams, and document new phish/spam techniques in Microsoft Azure VSO so the knowledge outlives the incident.

  • Close the loop. Findings feed back into ML, impersonation and spoof models rather than stopping at a single block.
